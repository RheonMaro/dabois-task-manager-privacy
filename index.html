DaBois' Task Manager — privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

DaBois' Task Manager is a community board for vlog quests, dates, categories, and priorities.

What the app stores

The app stores the quests, dates, category text and lists, priorities, progress amounts, and Trash entries that authorized community members save. It also stores record IDs, timestamps, versions, and a bounded set of save receipts containing the saving member's Root user ID, a request ID, and a hash of the request. Receipts prevent duplicate saves after timeouts; they do not contain a second copy of the quest text.

The app reads Root community ownership, membership, and role permissions to decide who may edit. The community owner and members with Manage Apps or Full Control may make changes. Other members with access to the app can view its board, including category content. Do not put passwords or private personal information into the board.

Where data is stored

Data is stored using Root's server-side app data store for the installed community. The app does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or a separate external database. Root operates the hosting and its own backups under Root's privacy policy.

Keeping and deleting data

Board entries remain until authorized editors remove them. Removing an entry sends it to Trash, where authorized editors may restore it or permanently delete it from the app's active workspace. Finished quests remain available until removed.

Save receipts can remain after an entry is deleted, until they are evicted from the latest 256 receipts. Deleting from the active workspace does not itself erase Root-managed backups; platform backup retention is controlled by Root.

Questions and requests

For privacy questions or requests about this app, contact RheonTechnology through Root. Community members can also contact their community owner about content saved in their community's board.

The app owner may update this policy when the app's behavior changes. Changes will be reflected on this page.
